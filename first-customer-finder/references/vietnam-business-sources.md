# Vietnam business source guide

Use this guide when the research scope targets companies operating in Vietnam. The source list is configurable per user and product. A directory establishes company identity; it does not, by itself, establish purchase intent.

## Source roles

### Company discovery

- Trang Vàng Việt Nam and other public B2B directories: discover companies by industry, product, location, and public business contact details.
- The National Business Registration Portal: corroborate the legal entity, enterprise code, address, legal status, and representative information when publicly available.
- Ministry of Industry and Trade Go Global directory: discover exporters, suppliers, and businesses listed by category and location.
- Official company websites and public business profiles: establish the canonical entity URL and current operating context.

### Business triggers

- Official company announcements, product launches, expansion notices, and hiring pages.
- Public job posts for operations, CRM, data, engineering, marketing, or customer-support workflows.
- Public funding and startup reporting, used as a timing signal rather than proof of buying intent.
- Public accelerator, trade-promotion, association, and business-event directories.

### Pain and intent

- Public LinkedIn posts and company pages.
- Public Facebook Pages, posts, and comments where the business intentionally discusses the problem.
- Public GitHub issues, discussions, and feature requests.
- Public forums, product reviews, and app marketplace reviews.
- Public requests for an agency, website, software, integration, CRM, automation, or internal tool.

### Contact route

- Official company contact page, website, business email, public company profile, public business page, or the original public discussion when its rules permit a reply.

## Qualification rules

- Require one company identity source and at least one separate pain, intent, or timing signal for a primary prospect.
- Treat funding, hiring, and directory presence as different signals. Do not merge them into a claim that the company wants to buy.
- Prefer official company routes over guessed personal contact details.
- Do not use private groups, gated sources, data brokers, scraped contact lists, personal email discovery, or phone enrichment.
- Record `signal_date` separately from `checked_at` and report blocked or snippet-only sources as coverage gaps.
- A public route is a possible connection path, not permission to send promotion.

## Search language

For Vietnam, search Vietnamese with diacritics, Vietnamese without diacritics, and English variants. Adapt terms to the user's target profile, for example `cần tìm đơn vị`, `đang tìm phần mềm`, `quy trình thủ công`, `kết nối API`, `quản lý bằng Excel`, `tuyển CRM`, `tự động hóa`, `mở rộng chi nhánh`, `gọi vốn`, and `tìm đối tác triển khai`.
